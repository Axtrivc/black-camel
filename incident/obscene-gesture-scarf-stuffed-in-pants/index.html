Obscene Gesture + Scarf Stuffed in Pants

Location Saudi Pro League
Category Off-pitch
Severity

The incident: On 25 February 2024, in the Saudi Pro League, Al Nassr edged Al Shabab 3-2 at home. After the final whistle, with away fans chanting 'Messi, Messi' to provoke him, Ronaldo first stuffed a fan's scarf into his pants, then made a grabbing, pumping gesture at his crotch — an obscene gesture captured by broadcast cameras and mobile phones alike.

Provocation and response: Background audio clearly captured the 'Messi' chants. With the Messi-Ronaldo grudge approaching its 20th year, for Saudi fans chanting 'Messi' is virtually the 'nuclear button' against Ronaldo. But responding to terrace taunts with a vulgar gesture was far beyond the bounds of 'venting emotion', and its coarseness shocked the global audience.

Not the first time: It was not Ronaldo's first 'crotch-grab' row in Saudi. ESPN noted that in April 2023, after Al Nassr's 0-2 loss to Al Hilal, Ronaldo was filmed grabbing his genitals on his way to the bench. The same gesture surfacing repeatedly across contexts forms a disturbing pattern, hardly accidental.

Heavy sanction: On 28 February 2024 the Saudi FA's disciplinary and ethics committee announced its verdict: Ronaldo was banned one match and fined a total of 30,000 riyals (10,000 to the FA, 20,000 paid to Al Shabab as complaint fees — about $8,000). The committee stressed the ruling was not subject to appeal, striking a firm tone.

Public outcry: The incident was widely covered by Reuters, ABC, The Guardian and other international outlets; criticism inside Saudi Arabia was sharpest. As the Pro League's top billboard star and a tourism ambassador, Ronaldo's behaviour was seen as disrespecting the host culture — in a society extremely sensitive to propriety and religious etiquette, crotch gestures are an untouchable taboo, far graver than a football matter.

Image blowback: Ironically, Ronaldo joined Saudi on a record salary (reportedly about €200M a year) tasked with 'lifting the league's global image'. Yet from the crotch-grab to the obscene gesture to the subsequent Saudi Super Cup elbow-red, a string of indiscretions has repeatedly put the Saudi league on global headlines for the wrong reasons — a major discount on the 'image project'.

⚠️ Disclaimer: This entry is compiled from 2024 public reporting by ESPN, Reuters, ABC News, The Athletic and The Guardian. The fine amount and ban matches follow the official announcement of the Saudi FA's disciplinary committee; the specific meaning of the on-site gesture is read differently depending on camera angle.
