Refused to Come On, Walked Out — Suspended & Fined by United

Location Old Trafford, Manchester, UK
Category Club & Law
Severity

What happened: On 19 October 2022, United beat Tottenham 2-0 in one of the most dominant displays of the Ten Hag era. Ronaldo, on the bench, barely warmed up; when Ten Hag called for him in the 87th minute, he point-blank refused; in stoppage time he rose, walked down the tunnel and left Old Trafford before the final whistle.

The club's hammer: The next day Ten Hag confirmed publicly: 'I asked him to come on and he refused.' United promptly dropped Ronaldo from the squad for the trip to Chelsea, made him train away from the first team, and imposed the standard fine of two weeks' wages — on his ~£500K weekly salary, roughly £720K to £1 million, Premier League record territory for a disciplinary fine.

The 'statement': On 20 October Ronaldo posted on Instagram that 'sometimes the heat of the moment gets the best of us' and that he had always acted with respect. Conspicuously absent: the word 'sorry' — no apology to the manager, the club, or the fans who had sung his name all night. It read as damage control, not contrition.

Not a first offence: On 31 July 2022, in his first game back (a friendly vs Rayo Vallecano), Ronaldo had already left the stadium early without permission; Ten Hag branded it 'unacceptable' at the time. October was the same script re-staged on league business — a repeat, and an escalation.

The backdrop: Ronaldo had spent the whole summer angling for the exit as Mendes hawked him around Europe's elite, and everyone said no (see the '2022 Transfer Farce' file). Being benched on his return was, to him, intolerable humiliation; his answer was not to win the place back on the pitch but to stage a bench-side protest for the world to see — a 37-year-old legend turning the substitutes' bench into his personal battlefield.

The second-to-last straw: Five weeks later came the Piers Morgan interview blasting United (see that file); days later the contract was terminated 'by mutual agreement' — Ronaldo arrived at the World Cup a free agent. The second marriage with Old Trafford was doomed from the moment that tunnel door swung shut.

Historic footnote: United fans will never forget the picture: the team playing its best football in years, the crowd in full voice — and the club's greatest-ever No.7 choosing to turn and walk into the dark tunnel. The self-styled 'winner's mentality' exiting like a loser: a watershed night in the collapse of the Ronaldo persona.

⚠️ Disclaimer:This file is compiled from Manchester United's official statements, Ten Hag's press conferences, and public reporting by Reuters, Sky Sports and The Athletic; the Instagram text is a translation of the public post. Fine figures are as reported in the media based on his wages (£720K–£1M).
