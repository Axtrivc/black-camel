Two COVID-Rule Breaches — A Privileged Quarantine

Location Turin, Italy ↔ Portugal ↔ Alpine valley
Category Off-pitch
Severity

First breach (October 2020): With Juventus in quarantine after two staff members tested positive, the entire squad was ordered to stay in Turin. Ronaldo flouted the order and flew to Portugal for the national-team camp, then tested positive for COVID-19. Italy's sports minister Spadafora publicly stated: 'I believe he breached the pandemic regulations.'

Flying home after the positive test: After the diagnosis, rather than isolate in place, Ronaldo took a private medical flight from Portugal back to Turin. Local health authorities said his action potentially violated the isolation order, and Italian police opened an investigation.

The anger over 'privileged passage': Ordinary citizens needed permits to cross regions, yet Ronaldo, thanks to his superstar status, moved freely between countries. Spadafora's public naming set off an Italian social-media firestorm — anger at 'rules not applying to megastars' was the hottest topic for days.

Second breach (January 2021): During a pandemic lockdown, Turin residents were barred from crossing regions. Yet Ronaldo took his girlfriend Georgina to an Alpine valley resort 100 kilometres away to celebrate her birthday. Police opened a probe for 'suspected breach of movement restrictions'; Ronaldo later pleaded 'personal reasons'.

Ronaldo's response: Confronted with the minister's call-out, Ronaldo countered that 'I did everything I was supposed to' and deflected blame onto Juventus and the health authorities. That 'I did nothing wrong, your rules are the problem' posture mirrors how he has handled every controversy since.

Broad comparison: In the same period several stars (e.g. Dybala, Rashford) strictly observed isolation after infection. Ronaldo's two breaches were seen by critics as a textbook case of 'using commercial value to hold the pandemic rules hostage' — what he wanted was not an exception but a conviction that he deserved one by default.

Verdict: Two public breaches of COVID rules, a row with a minister and a picture of privilege: stars like Cristiano could flout the restrictions the rest had to obey.

⚠️ Disclaimer:This file is compiled from public reporting by CGTN, The Guardian, ESPN, Al Jazeera, Goal.com and others. Both breaches were publicly confirmed / investigated by Italy's sports minister and police; Ronaldo denies the breach allegations.
