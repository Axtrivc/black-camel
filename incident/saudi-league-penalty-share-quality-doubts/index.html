Saudi-League Penalty Share — 'Quality' Doubts

Location Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
Category Club & Law
Severity

Saudi goals and the penalty share: According to Transfermarkt and multiple stats outlets, Ronaldo has taken over 32 penalties for Al Nassr. In the 2023/24 season he set a Saudi Pro League single-season record with 35 goals, but a hefty proportion came from 12 yards.

Penalty-merchant controversy: Stats show Al Nassr won 13 penalties in the 2024/25 season (converting 12), among the league leaders in spot-kicks. A team racking up that many penalties inevitably draws 'referee favouritism' and 'superstar dividend' suspicion; as the taker, Ronaldo naturally bears the brunt.

The quality question: Critics point out sharply that in a league far weaker defensively than Europe's, padding goal tally with lots of penalties raises real doubts about quality. A penalty hat-trick-style goal glut is one thing; dominating in open play is another.

The Messi-Miami contrast: The instinctive comparison is with Messi at Inter Miami. Messi has shone in MLS too, but mostly through playmaking and open-play creativity, not piling up penalties. That contrast sharply discounts the value of Ronaldo's 'Saudi top scorer' crown.

The league-level question: The deeper controversy is the league itself. The Saudi Pro League's level, tempo and intensity cannot compare with Europe's top five. Padding stats in such a league, then simply adding them to his European totals, is itself suspect of inflation.

Record versus actual level: Ronaldo has indeed used his Saudi-phase numbers to push his career goal tally to staggering heights. But when the 'all-time top scorer' crown is substantially built on penalties and weak opposition, the purity of that title deserves cool scrutiny.

The «Penaldo» nickname: In Saudi Arabia, the «Penaldo» nickname has only consolidated: many of his Saudi goals are penalties he himself wins or monopolises. The overall tally, inflated by penalties and minor opposition.

Verdict: Plenty of goals in Saudi Arabia, yes, but with a high share of penalties and against weaker defences. For the critics, Cristiano's Saudi «records» are proof of stat-padding and the modest quality of his goals.

⚠️ Disclaimer:Penalty-share data varies with the statistical method used, and judgements of league quality are subjective; this entry only presents the controversy.
